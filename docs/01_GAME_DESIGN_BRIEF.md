# Hellbindah Game Design Brief

## Core identity

Hellbindah is a Windows 11-first arcade 3D flight-combat game inspired by the feel of 1990s sci-fi shooters: fast free-flight, surface bases, tunnel infiltration, switches, hidden caches, reactor objectives, and dramatic extraction.

It must be an ode, not a clone. The game should use original names, story, characters, models, music, voice lines, and mission text.

## Vertical slice target

A 12-15 minute mission with these beats:

1. Briefing from ship computer.
2. Atmospheric insertion over hostile terrain.
3. Destroy transport ships and first turret cluster.
4. Follow compass to surface base.
5. Find/activate hatch switch.
6. Enter tunnel network.
7. Discover optional hidden cache.
8. Fight tunnel drones and turrets.
9. Destroy reactor shields.
10. Destroy reactor core.
11. Escape under timer.
12. Reach extraction/jump point.

## Core systems

- Arcade 6DOF ship movement with auto-level assist.
- Keyboard/mouse, controller, and future HOTAS bindings.
- Data-driven weapons and damage tags.
- Objective graph with dependencies, switches, doors, and checkpoints.
- Compass and nav computer.
- Hidden caches and score rewards.
- Timed escape sequence.
- Placeholder AI: fighter, turret, SAM.

## Success criteria

- New tester can complete the mission without developer instructions.
- Median first objective discovery time under 60 seconds.
- Player understands when a weapon is ineffective.
- Hidden cache is found by 30-50% of testers.
- 1080p/60 FPS target on a normal Windows 11 dev machine.
- No protected 1990s assets or content are used.
